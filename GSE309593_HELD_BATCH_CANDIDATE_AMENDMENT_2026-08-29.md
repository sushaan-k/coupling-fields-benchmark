# GSE309593 held-batch candidate amendment

The frozen 14-subject source cohort and nine-subject held cohort are unchanged. The split is defined by the numeric suffix of the seven deposited batch identifiers: B092, B099, B110, and B129 are used for source-only development; B162, B208, and B210 are held out.

This is a batch-ID holdout, not a chronological split. The v1 description of the source and held groups as earlier and later acquisitions was incorrect: for example, B099 was acquired in February 2024 and B162 in September 2023. This amendment removes the temporal interpretation before any numeric RNA or ADT value, cell identifier, association, or loss has been read.

The subject allocation, public-file bindings, axis-only preflight authorization, source and held decision rules, and obligation to publish every terminal or scored outcome remain unchanged.
