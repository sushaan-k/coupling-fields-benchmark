# Stephenson unused-Cambridge prediction recovery amendment v1.1

The first authorized RNA-margin prediction process was terminated by its
execution host after writing its exclusive attempt record and before writing a
prediction artifact. The surviving record is bound below. The lost process may
have opened metadata and read RNA ranges. Its command had no path that requested
ADT values, joined RNA to ADT, or formed outcome tables, so the interruption did
not expose a paired outcome.

This append-only amendment leaves the tagged v1 protocol, candidate
designation, authorization, and attempt unchanged. It permits one replacement
RNA-only attempt after the amendment is committed, tagged, verified from a
fresh public clone, and followed by a separate public authorization. The
replacement is attempt 2 and replacement 1 of 1. The original attempt is not
rerun. An exception after the replacement attempt is terminal.
